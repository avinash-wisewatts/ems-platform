## Status of this material

This is a **v0.1/v0.2 working product definition** — provisional in places,
and explicit about what is a decision vs. an inference vs. a genuinely open
question. The Product Owner Workshop baseline (Q1–Q101) is the primary
source of binding MVP decisions and is preserved, unedited, at
[../99-archive/superseded-product/ems-product-owner-workshop-baseline.md](../99-archive/superseded-product/ems-product-owner-workshop-baseline.md).
Material decisions extracted from it are recorded formally in
[../00-governance/decisions/](../00-governance/decisions/).
