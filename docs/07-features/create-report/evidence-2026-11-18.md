## E2. Timestamp semantics (Decision 1)

### E2.1 How EMS builds a 1-minute Energy row

`analytics.energy_consumption_1min` (behind `analytics.v_energy_consumption_native`,
read by `analytics.get_portal_asset_energy_series(..., '1m')`) stores per device
`bucket_start`, `previous_bucket_start`, `import_register_wh` and
`previous_import_register_wh`. Chiller1's device across the Saturday 17-Oct →
Sunday 18-Oct midnight (site local):

| bucket_start (local) | previous_bucket_start | register (Wh) | previous register (Wh) | delta (Wh) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-17 23:58 | 23:57 | 872,447,779.5 | 872,445,888.9 | 1,890.6 |
| 2026-10-17 23:59 | 23:58 | 872,449,635.6 | 872,447,779.5 | 1,856.1 |
| 2026-10-18 00:00 | 23:59 | 872,451,523.9 | 872,449,635.6 | 1,888.3 |
| 2026-10-18 00:01 | 00:00 | 872,453,381.5 | 872,451,523.9 | 1,857.6 |

Raw samples in `telemetry.normalized_points` for the same device:

| event_time (local) | ENERGY_IMPORT_TOTAL (Wh) | ACTIVE_POWER_TOTAL (W) |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-17 23:58:49 | 872,447,779.5 | 112,760 |
| 2026-10-17 23:59:49 | 872,449,635.6 | 112,690 |
| 2026-10-18 00:00:49 | 872,451,523.9 | 112,550 |
| 2026-10-18 00:01:49 | 872,453,381.5 | 112,850 |

**Fact:** the meter publishes one sample per minute, 49 s after the minute. EMS
bins each sample into the minute it falls in (`bucket_start` = the floor of
`event_time`). The Energy value of bucket *T* is the register at the sample in
*T* minus the register at the previous sample. Power at *T* is the instantaneous
sample in *T*, converted W → kW.

### E2.4 Conclusion and conversion rule

- For 1-minute Energy, Power and Temperature, EMS's label *T* (`bucket_start`)
  carries exactly the value Eniscope labels *T*. The tool established, from
  on/off transitions in the same October data, that Eniscope's *T* is the **end**
  of the metered interval. EMS's column is named `bucket_start`, but for this
  data the label marks the end of the metered minute.
- **Conversion:** `Timestamp (Site Local)` = `bucket_start` converted to the
  site timezone (`metadata.sites.timezone`, `Asia/Kolkata`), with no shift. The
  engine's existing interval-end attribution (`energy_period_day`) then applies
  unchanged.
- **Residual uncertainty (documented, not blocking):** the raw sample is stamped
  *T*+49 s. EMS cannot say whether the register snapshot was taken at *T* or at
  *T*+49 s. Either way the reported values equal the reference values, so
  reporting results are preserved. The only effect is a sub-minute question
  about where the physical day boundary sits.
- **Still to verify during implementation (planned tests T-MAP-2/3):** a
  full-period reconciliation of Power and Temperature (samples only so far). For
  the three 15-minute kitchen meters, that EMS 1-minute sums aggregate to the
  CSV's 15-minute values wherever the CSV has a value.

1-minute Temperature. A complete report is therefore possible only for periods
inside the **90-day** measurement window (and after the site's onboarding).
