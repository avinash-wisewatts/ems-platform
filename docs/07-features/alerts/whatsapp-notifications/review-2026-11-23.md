## 3. Provider desk research (P-1 to P-4): unverified, public sources only

Not a validation. Third-party sources were used where Meta's pages were
only partly retrievable. Each point must be confirmed against Meta's current
documentation and an actual account before Phase 2.

- **P-1 (opt-in, templates, pricing):**
  - **Opt-in:** Meta requires opt-in before business-initiated messages
    outside the 24-hour window. The opt-in must say the person agrees to
    receive messages from the business over WhatsApp, must name the
    business, and must comply with applicable law. A general (not
    WhatsApp-specific) opt-in has been accepted since a 2024 policy update.
  - **Templates:** messages outside the 24-hour window must use approved
    templates. Alerts would be **utility** templates.
  - **Pricing:** per delivered template message, by category and country.
    One third-party source (2025) put India utility at roughly ₹0.13 per
    message. Utility templates inside the service window reportedly became
    billable again from 1 Oct 2026. Re-verify against Meta's rate card.
- **P-2 (delivery receipts):**
  - **Signature:** webhooks are signed with HMAC-SHA256 over the raw body
    using the app secret, in `X-Hub-Signature-256` (`sha256=` prefix).
    Verify before parsing.
  - **Statuses:** `sent`, `delivered`, `read`, `failed`. Order is not
    guaranteed, and `delivered` may be skipped when `read` arrives.
    Deduplicate on (message id, status) and only move a status forward.
  - **New decision:** `read` is not in the PRD's status list and reveals
    recipient behaviour, so whether to store it is a decision for
    Product/legal.
- **P-3 (STOP):** Meta requires honouring opt-out requests made on or off
  WhatsApp. It provides no built-in STOP handling for utility messages:
  the business must detect the keyword in inbound messages (R10).
- **P-4 (template limits):**
  - The body is limited to 1,024 characters, variables included.
  - Variables may not contain tabs or newlines, or more than 4 consecutive
    spaces.
  - The body cannot start or end with a variable.

  This confirms C13: the worker must sanitise and truncate customer-entered
  names.
